Purchases and subscriptions

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy, open Sober Tracker, open the Plus screen (tap any locked feature, or Get Sober Tracker Plus in Settings) and tap Restore at the top.

How do I cancel the weekly subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, choose Sober Tracker and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Will I be charged during a free trial?

No. If the weekly plan shows a free trial, you are not charged when you cancel before it ends. If you don't cancel, the subscription renews at the weekly price shown in the app.

What is the Lifetime option?

A one-time purchase that unlocks Plus for good. It is not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. Restore it on your other devices with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
  1. On your old iPhone, open Settings › Back up now and save the file to iCloud Drive or the Files app.
  2. On the new iPhone, install Sober Tracker, open Settings › Restore from a backup and pick that file.

There is no account, so this backup file is how your history travels with you.

Can you recover my data if I deleted the app?

We never receive your data, so we can't restore it. If you saved a backup file, or your iPhone has an iCloud backup from before you deleted the app, you can restore from that.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings › Delete all data. This erases your streak, check-ins, cravings, habits, weigh-ins and your oak from the iPhone, and can't be undone. Deleting the app does the same.

Using the app

I had a drink. What happens to my streak?

On Home, under Had a drink?, choose what fits. It was a lapse keeps your streak going and marks the day. It's a relapse starts the counter fresh. Your milestones and badges stay on your path either way.

My daily reminder doesn't arrive
  1. In the app, open Settings › Reminders and make sure the daily check-in is on.
  2. On your iPhone, open Settings › Notifications › Sober Tracker and allow notifications.
  3. Check that a Focus mode isn't silencing the app.
How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for Sober Tracker and pick a size. The small streak widget is free; the other designs are part of Plus. If a widget shows a lock after you buy Plus, open the app once to refresh it.

Can I change what I used to drink or spend?

Yes. Your drinks per week and cost per drink are in Settings › Drinks & cost (part of Plus). Money, drinks and calories saved update straight away.
